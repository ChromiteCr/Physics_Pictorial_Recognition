    - issue: The 'must not change' and non-inferiority checks are underpowered and will fail regardless of the truth.
- PhyX TOST, +/-3 pp margin, n = 300: at 15% discordance the SE is sqrt(0.15/300) = 2.24 pp, so the 90% CI half-width is 3.7 pp, wider than the margin. Equivalence cannot be shown even with zero true change. 80% power at a true difference of 0 needs SE <= 1.03 pp, i.e. n of about 1,400.
